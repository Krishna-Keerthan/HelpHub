# Recovering a locked or inaccessible account

If you cannot sign in, work out which of three situations you are in.

Forgot password. Open the sign-in page and click Forgot password. Enter the email address you use for Acme Cloud and press Send reset link. The link is valid for 60 minutes and can be used once. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters that is not one of your last five. Requesting a new link makes the previous link stop working. Members who sign in with single sign-on reset their password with their company identity provider instead.

Account locked. After ten wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for 30 minutes. Wait and try again, or reset the password to unlock it sooner.

Lost second factor. If you lost the phone or the security key that generates your sign-in codes, use one of the ten backup codes you saved when you turned on two-factor authentication. Each code works once. If you have no backup codes, ask a workspace owner to turn off two-factor for your account, then set it up again. If you are the only owner, contact support: we verify your identity by asking for the billing email, the last four digits of the payment card and the workspace address, and the process takes up to three business days.
