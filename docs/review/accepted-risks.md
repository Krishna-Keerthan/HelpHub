| AR-20 | Production embeddings depend on Cloudflare Workers AI (`@cf/baai/bge-small-en-v1.5`, pooling "cls") | (1) Data leaves the system: every uploaded document's chunk text and every search or chat question is sent to Cloudflare for embedding. The quota behaviour was not exercised (deliberately not exhausted). (3) Vector compatibility with the stored vectors is checked by a log-only startup reference check (cosine >= 0.999) and an opt-in live test, not enforced at runtime. Cost visibility: `cf-ai-neurons` per call is logged (no content) | A paid plan or a second provider; or moving embeddings back in-process if the quota is hit in practice |

There is no retention or cleanup job, so rows accumulate | A public demo goes live, or a retention policy / cleanup job is added |
